# FreeBay social experience

Language for browsing FreeBay's social content and account destinations.

## Language

**Story**:
A time-limited image or video publication shown to its permitted audience. A story can also belong to a saved highlight.
_Avoid_: Feed post, reel

**Story viewer**:
The full-screen experience for watching stories from an author or a saved highlight.
_Avoid_: Story editor

**Feed drawer**:
The side menu reached from the feed, containing the signed-in user's summary and account destinations.
_Avoid_: Feed filters, bottom sheet

**Post conversation**:
A social post together with the responses associated with it, including responses to other replies.
_Avoid_: Chat conversation

**Post reply**:
A response to a social post or another reply within the same post conversation.
_Avoid_: Chat message

**Repost**:
A redistribution of an existing eligible post or reply without adding a new publication of one's own commentary.
_Avoid_: Quote, reply

**Quote post**:
A new publication containing the author's commentary and an embedded reference to an eligible original post or reply, with its own conversation.
_Avoid_: Repost with comment, reply to original

**Username**:
The public handle that identifies a FreeBay account, distinct from its display name.
_Avoid_: Display name, email

**Display name**:
The name an account presents to other people; it is not its unique public handle.
_Avoid_: Username

**Media item**:
One image, video, or GIF selected for editing or publication, with its own ordering, accessibility text, and—in chat—caption.
_Avoid_: Attachment collection, post

**Media collection**:
An ordered group of up to ten media items published together in a post, listing, or chat send. A collection may mix images and videos.
_Avoid_: Album when order or mixed media matters

**Cover item**:
The media item chosen to represent a media collection in compact surfaces. It remains part of the collection and does not duplicate the media.
_Avoid_: Thumbnail, first image

**Publication caption**:
Text describing a post as a whole, distinct from accessibility text and from a chat media item's caption.
_Avoid_: Alt text, item caption

**Alternative text**:
A concise accessibility description attached to one media item and not rendered as the publication caption.
_Avoid_: Caption

**Media hotspot**:
An accessible interactive region attached to a media item and aligned with a visible sticker. Link and location hotspots retain their destination after export; price and sold stickers are not hotspots.
_Avoid_: Sticker image, caption

**Meetup delivery**:
The v1 fulfilment model: buyer and seller hand over the item in person, and the buyer confirms receipt to release escrow.
_Avoid_: Shipping, posted delivery

**Verified account**:
An account whose owner has proven control of its email address. Required to sell, receive payouts, and place orders.
_Avoid_: Confirmed user, validated user

**Offer**:
A price proposal attached to a product conversation that the seller can accept or decline, producing a negotiated price for that buyer.
_Avoid_: Discount, coupon
